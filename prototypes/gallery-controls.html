Moving tiles (drag one while jiggling)

Hold a tile (or the empty background): the grid starts to wobble, a glass − appears on every tile, and the + turns into a ✓. Move before the hold lands and nothing happens — that's the scroll. Tap a − to delete; the grid closes up. Drag a tile to move it; the others make room. Once something has changed, a glass ↺ appears bottom-left to take it back. ✓ or a tap on the background settles everything. #jiggle in the URL starts in jiggle mode.
